Author

Phoenix T. Bird

Phoenix T. Bird is a licensed home inspector, an artist, and an author in the Tri-Cities of Washington, and the founder and publisher of H.D. Publishing.

Their first book, The Mirror Clause, is a review of one public family-court file, written in the manner of the classic detective novel and cited to the record throughout. It was the book that required the press to exist.

Alongside their own writing, they edit the house's Era Editions of public-domain classics and built the Writer, the manuscript editor the press uses for every title and now offers to first-time authors through the ninety-day program.

Inquiries about the author, including interviews and appearances, go to hello@hdpublishing.org.
