Privacy

What this site knows about you.

Very little, by design. This page describes hdpublishing.org itself. The bookshop and author accounts run on Shopify and are covered by the policies linked from those pages.

This website

hdpublishing.org is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no forms. If you choose a light or dark theme, that preference is kept in your own browser and never sent to us.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives the request, including your IP address, under its own privacy terms.

The site is served by our hosting provider, which keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, page requested, time) for operating the service.

Email

When you write to hello@ or support@, we keep the correspondence for as long as it is useful for answering you and for our records. We do not add you to a list, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

HDPublishingWriter

Books written in the app live on your own computer as plain files; we do not hold a copy. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code, so the account record is your email, your plan, and your assistant credit. When you use the assistant, the pages you choose are sent to Anthropic's Claude to answer, and the app shows you exactly which pages will go before you press Send; your private Journal is never included unless you choose it. Plans and credit packs are paid on Stripe's secure page, and we never see your card number.

The bookshop

Purchases and downloads of our books are handled by Shopify on our behalf at the shop address. Shopify's handling of your order data is described in its own privacy policy and in the policies shown at checkout. We see what a shopkeeper sees: your order, your email, and your delivery address for a paperback.
