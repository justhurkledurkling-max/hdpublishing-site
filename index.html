THE LONG TABLE

The table came before the house did. My grandfather built it in a rented garage the winter they were married, out of two doors and a beam he had been given for hauling away, and it moved with them eleven times before it stopped.

I learned to write at that table, which is to say I learned to wait at it. Bread rose under a towel at one end. Bills waited at the other.

Assistant (suggestion): the chapter opens on the table and ends on the bills. Consider letting the towel come back once, near the end. Accept · Reject

Nobody in the family remembers who first called it the long table, only that no one has ever called it anything else.

Everything a book needs

From first page to finished book.

Planning, writing, editing, proofing and publishing, in one app built around how books are really made.

Write on the printed page

You write on a sheet that looks like your finished page, with a familiar Word-style toolbar. No code, no clutter.

Your book is a folder

Every page is a plain file on your own computer. Back it up, move it, keep it in Dropbox or iCloud. Writing works offline.

Proof Book

See the whole book laid out as printed pages, with page numbers and running heads, plus a Kindle preview on phone, tablet and e-reader.

Ready for Amazon KDP

Print-ready paperback, hardcover and spiral-bound PDFs and a Kindle EPUB, with a list of anything Amazon would reject.

Cover designer

Lay out the front, spine and back from your book's details, with the bleed, safe area and barcode space marked.

Work with your editor

Send the manuscript to your editor, review their changes page by page, and see every version on a shared dashboard.

Optional AI assistant

An assistant that suggests, and never takes over.

Powered by Claude from Anthropic. Ask about a line, a chapter or the whole book. Every change arrives as a suggestion you accept or reject.

  • It never changes your text by itself.
  • Before you press Send, you see exactly which pages will go.
  • Your private Journal stays out unless you choose to include it.
  • Ready-made reviews of the whole book, kept with your Production Notes.
  • Optional: the app works fully without it.
How it works

From first draft to published book.

1

Start a book

Pick a size, binding and font, or bring in the draft you already have. Title, copyright and contents pages fill themselves in.

2

Plan and write

Keep brain dumps in a private Journal and timelines and characters in Production Notes. A writing challenge keeps the pace.

3

Edit together

Send the book to your editor. Their changes come back as suggestions, and colored pages show what still needs attention.

4

Proof and publish

Check every page in Proof Book, then make the print PDF and Kindle eBook that Amazon KDP asks for.
