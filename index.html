The Writer

Ninety days from an idea to a printed book.

A manuscript editor built for books rather than documents, a weekly structure that keeps you writing, and a press at the end of it. You write every sentence. The software keeps the shape, shows you the real pages as you go, and produces the paperback and ebook files when you are done.

  • You write the book. AI helps with the outline at the start and a developmental read at the end. It never writes your prose.
  • See the pages while you write. Proof your book as real 6 × 9 spreads or as a reflowable ebook at any point.
  • Published by H.D. A finished 6 × 9 paperback and EPUB, with an ISBN, under this imprint unless you ask otherwise.

THE LONG TABLE

The table came before the house did. My grandfather built it in a rented garage the winter they were married, out of two doors and a beam he had been given for hauling away, and it moved with them eleven times before it stopped.

I learned to write at that table, which is to say I learned to wait at it. Bread rose under a towel at one end. Bills waited at the other.

Editor's note (week 6): the chapter opens on the table and ends on the bills. Consider letting the towel come back once, near the end.

Nobody in the family remembers who first called it the long table, only that no one has ever called it anything else.

How we work

The standards behind the seal.

  • Human authorship. Our authors write their own books. Where we use AI at all, it is for structure and critique, and it is never used to generate the text you read.
  • Everything cited. In our nonfiction, every fact, quotation, and date is cited to its source by date and index. Interpretation is marked as interpretation.
  • First-printing texts. Era Editions follow the book's first printing. Variants are noted; nothing is silently modernised. The editorial apparatus is marked as ours.
  • Paper first. Every title is designed as a 6 × 9 book before it is anything else. The ebook follows the print, not the other way round.
  • Open type. Interiors are set in openly licensed typefaces we can name in the colophon and ship with the files.
  • Small, on purpose. A handful of titles a year, each one finished. We would rather publish less and stand behind all of it.
