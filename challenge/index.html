THE LONG TABLE

The table came before the house did. My grandfather built it in a rented garage the winter they were married, out of two doors and a beam he had been given for hauling away, and it moved with them eleven times before it stopped.

I learned to write at that table, which is to say I learned to wait at it. Bread rose under a towel at one end. Bills waited at the other.

Assistant (suggestion): the chapter opens on the table and ends on the bills. Consider letting the towel come back once, near the end. Accept · Reject

Nobody in the family remembers who first called it the long table, only that no one has ever called it anything else.

Questions

About the challenge

Who is the challenge for?

Anyone with a book in them and a reason to finish it: a first novel, a memoir, a guide or a workbook.

What if I fall behind?

Today's goal is what is left, shared over the days left. Miss a day and the days after it each take a little of it, so you are never behind by more than today can fix.

Do I need to know how to publish?

No. Proofing, the cover and the files Amazon KDP asks for are all part of the program.

What happens to my plan afterwards?

The challenge includes Pro for its 90 days. Afterwards your account is Limited, with the app, unlimited books and the publish-ready files, unless you keep the Pro subscription.
