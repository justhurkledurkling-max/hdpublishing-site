Writing in four steps.

1

Download and install

Open the installer and drag HDPublishingWriter into your Applications folder.

2

Sign in with your email

No password to remember: the app emails you a six-digit code. Once signed in, you can write offline.

3

Take the tour

A short guided tour points out the sidebar, the page, Proof Book and the assistant.

4

Start your book

Choose New Book, or point the app at the chapters you already have.
